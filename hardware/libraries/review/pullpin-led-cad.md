JST source: [official GH drawing, pp2–3](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eGH.pdf), [product/CAD index](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/index.php?lang=2&series=105). Exact manufacturer's STEP download is now an email-request form requiring company/contact details; no request was submitted. The verified local stock model is the qualified fallback. Both copied model files retain KiCad's CC-BY-SA4 license and design-use exception.

The matching GH harness uses`GHR-02V-S` housing and`SSHL-002T-P0.2` contacts, AWG26–30. Series rating is1A with AWG26,50V,−40…+105°C including temperature rise. The pull-pin circuit draws only divider current and does not approach that rating. This GH part was an earlier library option and is not the selected carrier pull-pin connector. The carrier uses XT30PW-M with J6.1 directly connected to LM74502 EN/UVLO and J6.2 to GND. Closed COM-NO contacts inhibit power; an open or unplugged contact permits power subject to UVLO. The mechanism must press the lever and close COM-NO with the pin inserted, then release it when the pin is removed. Confirm the physical contact states. Mechanical pads are not switch contacts.

LiteOn source: manufacturer-authored **DS22-2000-073 RevH, effective2017-11-28**, packagep1 and land patternp6. [Manufacturer URL](https://optoelectronics.liteon.com/upload/download/DS22-2000-073/LTST-C170KGKT.PDF) currently returned HTML to this session, so the same manufacturer PDF was obtained from the [datasheets.com mirror](https://www.datasheets.com/lite-on/ltst-c170kgkt/datasheet.pdf). Its cover, exact part, revision, package drawing and land drawing were visually inspected. Saved source is`build/carrier-completion/root-evidence/LTST-C170KGKT-manufacturer-mirror.pdf`; the failed manufacturer response is preserved separately and was not treated as a PDF. [Exact DigiKey listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/liteon/LTST-C170KGKT/386777):160-1414-1-ND, MPN LTST-C170KGKT. Stock is not reserved.
