# Final local CAD review

Reviewed 2026-10-02. **The selected footprints load in KiCad and their saved model transforms render upright, with contacts on the intended pad sides.** This review made no library, schematic, project or actual-PCB writes. It created only a build coupon/evidence and this report. The GH side-entry representative models have a height limitation recorded below.

|---|---|---|

All 16 lands checked: pin 1 upper-left, pin 15 upper-right, 6/10 lower corners, 7/8/9 lower-middle. Central narrow pad 16 is SW, not GND. Land 16 is 0.35×2.975 mm centred at (0,−0.9625) mm; ordinary 0.25 mm pads use 0.5 mm pitch. Only the 1 mm/2 mm bare test-point pads intentionally have no model. This is a dependency check, **not geometric verification of all 52 parts**. J3's local derived model now resolves; this review did not independently validate its geometry.
